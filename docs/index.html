A new era of autonomous shuttles

Meet Surro

Surro is an autonomous shuttle project fully developed in the UAE, representing the next generation of intelligent urban mobility.

Surro is being developed by MindShield in close collaboration with Khalifa University's Autonomous Vehicle Lab (AVLab), UAE's first research lab dedicated to advancing autonomous vehicles for real-world deployment.

Our mission is to deliver safe, reliable, and scalable autonomous transportation solutions that transform how people and goods move through cities.
